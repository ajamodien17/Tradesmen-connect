01 · THE GAP

Getting your first job shouldn't require already having one.

Most platforms verify people who are already established — a license, a rating history, years on the job. That works well for experienced pros. It works against everyone else.

FOR GRADS

You finished the diploma. You still can't get hired, because every listing wants "2+ years experience."

FOR SELF-TAUGHT WORKERS

You learned on real job sites, from real tradespeople — but there's no certificate for that, and no platform that shows it.

FOR EMPLOYERS

You'd take a chance on someone new — if there were a low-risk way to actually do it.

03 · HOW IT WORKS

Built to give someone a real first shot.

TRIAL JOBS

A low-risk way in

Short, defined jobs employers post specifically for entry-level and building-tier workers. Real pay, real work, no long commitment either way.

MENTOR PAIRING

Learn on real jobs

Licensed workers can take on an apprentice for supervised jobs. Every job completed together builds job history and comes with a real endorsement.

HONEST PROFILES

No guessing games

Employers see exactly what's behind every badge — a license number, or the specific jobs and endorsements that earned the tier.
